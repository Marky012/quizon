================================================================================
PLAYER ICON PROMPTS - GENDER SELECTION (BOY / GIRL)
Two bust icons for the Profile screen's "Who are you?" panel.
================================================================================

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
--------------------
The two player icons in public/assets/players/ are the only character art in
this product that does NOT follow the art bible. Everything else - the eight
pups, the brand mark, the level cards - is flat 2D vector with a bold 3px navy
outline and a closed nine-colour palette. boy.svg and girl.svg were drawn
earlier, by hand, in a different style:

  - No outline at all. The shapes are separated by value, which is why the
    girl reads as a pink blob on a cream card and the boy as a brown blob.
  - Off-palette skin (#f7c193), hair (#4a2c1a), blush (#ff9bb0) and pure white
    eye dots, applied over off-palette turquoise and coral.
  - A perfectly circular head, which is a helmet, not a child.

The design principles the rest of the product holds to are stated in
src/index.css (the token block) and in PUP_PROMPTS.txt (the set contract).
This file applies the same rules to the two player icons, so the pupil's own
face belongs to the same world as the eight dogs that teach them.

WHAT THESE ICONS HAVE TO DO
----------------------------
  1. THEY ARE READ AT TWO SIZES. 120px in the picker card, 72px inline next to
     the name field. So they are BUSTS - head and shoulders - and not full
     body figures. A full body figure at 72px is a torso with no face on it.
  2. THEY MUST BE DISTINGUISHABLE WITHOUT COLOUR. The product rule is "never
     colour alone" (README, Accessibility). So the boy and the girl are
     separated by HAIR SILHOUETTE first and shirt colour second. If you grey
     the two icons out and swap the shirts, they must still read as different.
  3. THEY ARE NOT THE PUPS. The pupils are dogs in caps and vests. These two
     are children, plain, with no hat, no prop, no toy, no accessory beyond
     the girl's hair ties. Anything else on the head would fight the hair
     silhouette, which is the thing doing the identifying.
  4. THEY ARE FILLED IN BY CODE, NOT BY THE IMAGE. No writing, no badge, no
     letter, no number anywhere - not on the shirt either.

================================================================================
HOW TO USE
================================================================================
  1. Paste ONE block into your image generator. One icon at a time, never
     both in one image.
  2. Save the result under the SAVE AS name in that block's header.
  3. Run it against MUST PASS at the bottom before you accept it. If it
     fails, re-roll. Do not patch it by hand.
  4. Generate the boy and the girl with the SAME seed and the SAME settings
     if your tool allows it. The two must be the same drawing with two
     different heads on top, not two different drawings.

  OUTPUT FORMAT: a square vector with a 0 0 200 200 viewBox and a fully
  transparent background, OR a 512x512 PNG with a transparent background.
  The SVG drops straight into the code as-is. A PNG needs one edit: the two
  paths in src/screens/Profile.tsx (the CHARACTERS array) change .svg to .png.

  THE SHIPPED ART IS PNG, AND THE EXTENSION MUST MATCH THE CONTENT. The two
  icons in public/assets/players/ are 512x512 PNGs and the code asks for
  .png. Do NOT save a PNG under a .svg name: a PNG that is called an SVG is
  served with the wrong Content-Type, and the next person to open the file
  finds a PNG header where an <svg> tag should be.

================================================================================
THE SET CONTRACT - READ THIS ONCE, IT IS THE SAME FOR BOTH ICONS
================================================================================

CANVAS AND FRAMING - THE PART THAT IS HARD TO GET RIGHT
  A square canvas, viewBox 0 0 200 200, straight-on eye-level view, dead
  symmetrical. The child faces the viewer directly. The head is centred
  horizontally at x = 100 and sits high in the frame, occupying roughly the
  top 55% of the height. The head is BIG - about 100 units across - because
  the icon is read at 72px and a small head vanishes. The neck and the
  shoulders enter from the bottom edge and are CROPPED by it. You see the
  head, the neck, the shoulders and the top of the shirt. You do NOT see the
  body, and you do NOT see arms below the shoulder line.

FACE
  A child's face, not an adult's and not a baby caricature. Large round
  friendly eyes with big dark navy pupils and ONE small white highlight dot
  in each eye. Simple round nose. A small closed smile with rounded ends.
  Two soft round blush ovals on the cheeks. Eyebrows are optional; if they
  appear they are short, soft and curved, never angled. The expression is
  calm, friendly and slightly curious.

OUTLINE - THIS IS THE STYLE, DO NOT SKIP IT
  A bold 3px dark navy #1A2B4C outline on EVERY contour: the hair, the face,
  the ears, the neck, the shoulders, the shirt, and every interior line -
  the smile, the nose, the eyelids, the hair parting - at the same 3px
  weight. Every corner in the drawing is ROUNDED. No sharp corners anywhere,
  not in the hair, not in the shirt, not in the shoulders.

STYLE
  Flat 2D vector illustration for a children's educational reading game.
  Chunky rounded shapes. Flat solid fills only: no gradients, no shading, no
  glow, no rim light, no soft blur, no texture, no drop shadow on the
  outline. No background of any kind - the card behind the icon supplies it.

PALETTE - THE GAME'S NINE VALUES, PLUS FOUR HUMAN VALUES
  The game palette is exactly nine values and nothing else: cream #FFFDF4,
  indigo #1A237E, navy #1A2B4C, turquoise #00D2C4, deep turquoise #00A396,
  lime #4BD37B, coral #FF5E62, yellow #FFD13B, violet #7B61FF.

  A child needs a skin tone and a hair colour and the nine game values do
  not contain either, so the human figures use four MORE values and no
  others: skin #F7C193, hair #4A2C1A, blush #FF9BB0, and pure white #FFFFFF
  used ONLY for the single small highlight dot in each eye and nowhere else
  in the image. The skin tone is a warm tan, the skin tone of a child in
  the Caraga region, and it is the same tan in both icons.

  The boy wears turquoise #00D2C4 and the girl wears coral #FF5E62, matching
  the two icons already in the product, so the picker card and the shirt
  stay in step with the rest of the app.

================================================================================
MUST PASS - CHECK BOTH FILES BEFORE YOU ACCEPT THEM
================================================================================
  1. NO WRITING ANYWHERE. Check the shirt especially. One letter fails it.
  2. THE OUTLINE IS ON EVERY CONTOUR - hair, ears, jaw, neck, shoulder, shirt
     and collar. If you can trace a shape that has no navy line around it, it
     fails.
  3. THE OUTLINE IS 3PX AND UNBROKEN. No thin lines, no gaps at the collar,
     no gaps where the hair meets the shoulder.
  4. FLAT FILLS ONLY. Turn off colour and the image should be a set of solid
     shapes with clean edges, not a picture with light on it.
  5. TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND. Put it on a checkerboard. Any white or coloured
     field behind the figure fails it, because the picker card paints its own
     cream or turquoise behind the icon.
  6. IT IS A BUST. Head, neck, shoulders, cropped at the bottom edge. No
     hands, no arms, no full body.
  7. THE FACE IS SYMMETRICAL AND THE HEAD IS THE SAME SIZE IN BOTH ICONS.
     Overlay the two. The heads must line up and the eye line must be at the
     same height in both, or the picker cards will look broken.
  8. AT 72PX, THE FACE STILL READS: two eyes, a nose, a mouth. If the eyes
     are gone, the head is too small.
  9. GREYSCALE TEST. Desaturate both icons and swap the shirts. They must
     still be obviously different, because the girl is told apart by her hair
     and not by her coral shirt.
 10. BOY AND GIRL ARE NOT MIRRORED COPIES. Same drawing, same pose, same
     camera, different hair and different shirt colour.
 11. NO SHARP CORNERS ANYWHERE.
 12. THE ICON STILL READS AT 120px AND AT 72px. Check both sizes.

================================================================================
NOTES
================================================================================
  - NO SELECTED-STATE ART. The picker draws the selection itself: a
    turquoise card, a 4px yellow ring and a navy check badge in the corner
    (src/screens/Profile.tsx). A second pair of "selected" icons would
    duplicate that and drift out of step with the ring.
  - NO LABEL ART. The word "Boy" or "Girl" is set in Baloo 2 in the code
    under the icon, and the button carries an aria-label. The picture is not
    the only channel.
  - IF THE TOOL WILL NOT OUTPUT A TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND, cut the figure out
    by hand and keep the alpha. A white rectangle behind a cream-shirted
    child on a cream card is invisible, and this is the same failure Franky
    the Pug has.
  - THE ICONS ARE SAVED OVER THE TWO EXISTING FILES. The new art has a navy
    outline and the old art did not, so the picker will look noticeably
    crisper. That is the point.
  - A DARKER SKIN TONE is a two-value edit and nothing else: replace skin
    #F7C193 in both blocks, and nothing else changes. The two icons must
    always use the SAME skin tone as each other, whatever it is.
  - The store calls these 'boy' and 'girl' (src/store/gameStore.ts,
    PlayerGender) and the sync payload deliberately does NOT carry the
    choice off the device. Nothing in this file changes that.
